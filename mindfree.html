FAQ

Questions We Hear A Lot

Are the first two classes really free — no hidden fees?
Yes, completely free. Classes 1 and 2 require no credit card, no account — just your email. We believe everyone deserves access to the foundational tools. You only pay if you choose to continue from Class 3 onwards.
Can this replace therapy or medication?
These classes teach evidence-based techniques that are used in therapy — but they're not a clinical treatment and don't replace professional care. If you're in crisis or on medication, please speak to your doctor. Many students use MindFree alongside therapy and find it significantly accelerates their progress.
How long does each class take?
Each class is 45–75 minutes of video, plus a downloadable workbook you can complete at your own pace. Most students do one class per week, which gives time to practise the techniques before moving on. There's no timer — go at whatever pace feels right.
What if I've had anxiety or depression for years?
Many of our most dramatic transformations come from people who've struggled for 10, 15, even 20+ years. Long-term anxiety and depression are often deeply habitual patterns — and patterns can be changed. The length of time you've struggled doesn't predict how quickly you can shift. Start with Class 1 and see for yourself.
Is there a refund policy?
Yes — 30-day full refund, no questions asked, for any paid class or the full series. We're confident enough in the material that we take all the risk.
