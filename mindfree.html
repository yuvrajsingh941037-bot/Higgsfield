FAQ

Questions We Hear A Lot

Are the first two classes really free — no hidden fees?
Yes, completely free. Classes 1 and 2 require no credit card, no payment of any kind — just your email to create a free account. You only pay if you choose to continue from Class 3 onwards.
Can this replace therapy or medication?
These classes teach evidence-based techniques used in therapy — but they're not a clinical treatment and don't replace professional care. Many students use MindFree alongside therapy and find it significantly accelerates their progress.
What if I've had anxiety or depression for years?
Many of our most dramatic transformations come from people who've struggled for 10, 15, even 20+ years. Long-term anxiety and depression are deeply habitual patterns — and patterns can be changed. Start with Class 1 and see for yourself.
How long does each class take?
Each class is 45–75 minutes of video, plus a downloadable workbook. Most students do one class per week. There's no timer — go at whatever pace feels right for you.
